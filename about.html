The Glazeworks Story

Founded in 2018, Glazeworks began as a passion project between two architects turned pastry chefs. We noticed a parallel between architectural precision and the art of French patisserie.

Every curve, line, and layer in our pastries is intentionally designed. We apply geometric principles to create pastries that are not only visually stunning but also structurally perfect.
